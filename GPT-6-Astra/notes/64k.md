# THE LOOM — 64k implementation

Build from the project root with `bash src/64k/build.sh`.
The script embeds both GLSL sources, builds and strips a native ELF, removes
the Nix compiler's build-time RPATH, verifies that the payload fits, and pads
the executable to exactly 65,536 bytes. The executable uses the supplied
`/lib64/ld-linux-x86-64.so.2` loader and SDL2, GL, libm, and libc sonames.
It does not self-extract, execute another program, or read production files.

The artwork moves from incomplete metallic fragments (0–12 seconds), through
closing, weaving copper and teal cables (12–28), into a nested armillary
sculpture with a golden spherical cage, radial needles, engraved surfaces,
and a luminous core (28–49). The final held form and custom vector MORPH
lettering resolve between 49 and 60 seconds. The final three seconds fade
to black. Escape or the window close event exits cleanly.

An OpenGL 3.3 ray marcher renders into a 1920×1080 RGBA16F buffer. A second
pass adds two bloom scales, tone mapping, vignette, grain, vector typography,
and cinematic framing. The presentation is fitted to every drawable while
preserving its aspect ratio. All geometry, lights, dust, and background
fields are generated mathematically in the embedded shaders.

The original score is synthesized during startup at 48 kHz stereo. Its
thirty-bar, 120 BPM structure develops slowly moving chord pads, struck
glass arpeggios, a call-and-response melody, plucked bass, synthesized
kick/snare/hat voices, and diffuse stereo feedback delays. The final major
third provides a harmonic resolution. Samples are normalized with peak
headroom and queued directly to SDL; there are no prerecorded samples.
Visual light pulses follow the same kick subdivision and composition clock.

Development options (none required for normal playback):

- `--windowed`: use a normal 1920×1080 window.
- `--mute`: skip audio generation and output.
- `--time 40 --capture build/64k/frame.ppm`: render a deterministic, silent
  still, write a PPM into the requested path, and exit.
- `--audio build/64k/score.wav`: generate a 60-second 16-bit stereo PCM WAV
  into the requested path and exit without opening a window.

Initial verification: compiled and captured the production at 3, 23, 40,
and 54 seconds; checked visibly distinct states and the legible MORPH title.
Generated audio contains 2,880,000 stereo sample frames (60 seconds), with
headroom and no clipping. The root audit verifies complete fullscreen
playback, actual audio output, timing, final size, and Escape behavior.
