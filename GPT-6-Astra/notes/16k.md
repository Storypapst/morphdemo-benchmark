# 16 KiB: Crystal Assembly

The scene evolves continuously from scattered, rotating mineral fragments into a faceted eightfold sculpture and a pair of fine armillary orbits. Cold silver surfaces carry blue and warm reflected light; procedural dust converges on the ordered object. Embedded five-by-seven letter masks spell MORPH at the opening and resolution.

The score is synthesized continuously in the SDL audio callback at 48 kHz stereo. Its progression is D minor, B-flat major, F major, C7, with swelling sine pads, an eight-note pulse/arpeggio, bass, synthesized kick/snare/hat and cross-fed delay. The visual illumination pulse and traveling highlights share its 120 BPM timeline. Graphics follow an atomic audio sample cursor, with between-buffer interpolation, so device startup latency does not displace the beat. The audiovisual timeline is 60 seconds, with onset and final fades; Escape and window close terminate cleanly.

`bash src/16k/build.sh` builds the final executable plus a development executable under `build/16k`. The build minifies the embedded shader, links against the supplied SDL2/OpenGL/math runtime sonames, removes unused ELF section-header metadata and pads the native ELF to 16,384 bytes. It performs no self-decompression and creates no runtime files. No production assets, fonts or scene files are read. All GLSL and compact music/letter tables are embedded in the executable.

Development executable only: `MORPH_WINDOWED=1 MORPH_TIME=36 build/16k/debug` begins at a chosen timeline position. The final executable does not read these variables.

Offline synthesis verification: 60 seconds rendered at 48 kHz; RMS 0.06046, peak 0.50946, no clipping. Full display/audio, duration and Escape verification is coordinated by the root agent.
